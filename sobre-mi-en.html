Who am I?

I am Matías Gaglio, a data and communication professional specialized in ecommerce, listing management, SEO, PPC, Business Intelligence and performance analysis. I work at the intersection between commercial strategy, data reading and visual communication.

My experience focuses on detecting specific growth problems: loss of traffic, low conversion, poorly structured campaigns, unclear listings, profitability problems or decisions made without sufficient evidence. My value is in translating scattered data into simple, actionable decisions.

I am especially interested in the point where human behavior, communication, and algorithmic systems intersect. That's why my portfolio combines ecommerce, visuals, opinion research, automation and cultural analysis.
