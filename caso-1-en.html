Conversion Rate Optimization in Amazon Ads

Restructuring campaigns, improving listings and translating data into profitable growth decisions.

Listing Management & Data

The problem

The portfolio had traffic and sales, but was limited by an overly defensive advertising structure and by listings that did not communicate the technical value of the product clearly enough. The problem wasn't just bidding: it was a disconnect between search, content, image, and conversion.

The audit showed a clear opportunity: separate the PPC funnel, improve keyword reading and convert the gallery into a more informative visual system.

Intervention

what did I do

I reorganized campaigns separating exact, phrase, broad and auto for harvesting. The intention was to stop treating all terms as if they had the same value and to start distinguishing between exploration, defense, conversion and escalation.

In parallel, I worked on titles, bullets and images to reduce cognitive friction: less keyword stuffing, more understandable phrases, more visible technical compatibility and a structure designed for shoppers who compare quickly.

  • +35% Sales
  • +15% Conversion
  • +50% ROAS

The logic was simple: if the listing can already convert, PPC should bring qualified traffic. If PPC finds profitable terms, the listing must absorb them without losing clarity. The result is a learning system, not isolated optimization.
