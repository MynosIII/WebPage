the meme of Milei Chad

How humor shapes political perception in the age of AI-generated images under the veil of laughter.

Author
Matías Ignacio Gaglio
Publication
December 2025 — May 2026
Institution
University of Buenos Aires
DOI
10.62174/rs.10864
Summary

The article analyzes the “Milei Chad” meme as a device of political aestheticization in digital culture. From an interdisciplinary perspective that articulates semiotics, visual studies and theory of affectivity, it examines how images—often generated with artificial intelligence—transform Javier Milei into a hypermasculine icon.

The research reconstructs the genealogy of the “Chad” archetype, its circulation from the online machosphere and the way in which humor, digital reproducibility and algorithmic logic can naturalize political values and shift the debate towards an affective spectacle.
