Animation 03 · Kinetic typography · 4s

Be You · Kinetic type

I approached this as a typography and timing exercise in After Effects. The words are separated into text layers so weight, position and opacity can change independently. Instead of adding decorative effects, I used the rhythm of the edit, a precise baseline and a controlled hold on the final message to create the emotional shift.

03 — After Effects build

A complete emotional turn in four seconds.

01

Type construction

I kept the typography live in After Effects and split the sentence into independently controlled text layers. A shared guide layer locked the baseline and optical center, while separate weights let “perfect” carry the initial tension before the visual hierarchy changes.

02

Text animators

Opacity and position animators are driven by range selectors rather than manual letter-by-letter keyframes. That produces a cleaner progressive reveal and makes it easy to adjust the speed of the phrase without rebuilding the animation.

03

The transition

The first statement exits with a short directional move and a rapid opacity taper. “Be You” enters on a contrasting beat with a small scale overshoot; the motion curve accelerates through the change and settles gently so the transformation feels decisive.

04

Negative space and hold

The final words stay isolated in the center with no competing graphic elements. I reserved the longest hold for this frame and kept the background pure black, giving the viewer enough time to read the message before the loop resets.
