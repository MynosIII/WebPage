Amazon Listing Audit Checklist

What to verify before increasing ad spend: a complete diagnostic sequence for separating discoverability, conversion, catalog, operational, and profitability problems.

Start here

Do not optimize the visible copy before proving the listing can sell.

A listing audit is a dependency check. Advertising cannot compensate for a suppressed ASIN, a lost Buy Box, unavailable inventory, weak delivery promise, broken variation, or an offer that cannot convert profitably. The first task is to identify which layer is constraining performance.

1. Retail readinessLive, purchasable, in stock, compliant.
2. RelevanceIndexed for the right demand.
3. ConversionClear, credible, competitive.
4. EconomicsMargin supports acquisition.
01 / Retail readiness

Can the shopper actually complete the purchase?

Listing status

Confirm the ASIN is active, searchable, category-correct, and free from suppression or detail-page removal.

Buy Box and offer

Check featured-offer eligibility, price consistency, seller status, and whether another offer owns the conversion path.

Inventory and fulfillment

Review FBA/FBM availability, inbound stock, stranded units, delivery promise, and imminent stockout risk.

Variation integrity

Verify parent-child theme, child labels, swatches, review sharing, and that each variation matches the customer's selection logic.

02 / Search relevance

Does Amazon understand when this product belongs?

Keyword map

Prioritize the intersection of demand and relevance. Separate category heads, product attributes, use cases, audiences, and long-tail modifiers.

Title logic

Lead with product identity and decision-critical attributes. Preserve human syntax while making the primary search intent unmistakable.

Bullet coverage

Map one buyer question to each bullet: configuration, compatibility, use, construction, care, guarantee, or proof.

Backend and indexation

Check missing synonyms, duplicate terms, restricted claims, category attributes, and manual indexation for priority queries.

From the operating handbook: high volume alone is not enough; the target is the intersection of volume and relevance, with long-tail terms useful in highly competitive markets.
03 / Conversion

Can a shopper resolve uncertainty without leaving the page?

04 / Performance diagnosis

Use the funnel to locate the loss.

Impressions down

Investigate indexation, organic rank, search share, PPC coverage, category placement, stock, and suppression.

Clicks down

Compare main image, title, price, coupon, rating, delivery promise, badge, and competing offers on the SERP.

Conversion down

Inspect detail-page communication, reviews, returns, price, offer stability, variation clarity, and competitor changes.

Profit down

Reconcile price, fees, landed cost, ad spend, returns, reimbursements, promotions, and inventory carrying costs.

The archived product review reinforces this sequence: a contribution-margin decline was checked against stock, FBA fees, units, revenue, reimbursements, organic/paid mix, return rate, keyword rank, conversion, and PPC before actions were proposed. That breadth prevents one metric from becoming a premature diagnosis.

Anonymized audit evidence

A margin alert required reconciliation, not a copy rewrite.

In one archived product audit comparing July with August 2024, units declined from 4,105 to 2,049, revenue moved from $73,631.43 to $36,774.97, and contribution margin fell from $14,529.98 to $5,281.59. The percentage changes are calculated as (August − July) ÷ July: -50.1% for units, -50.1% for revenue, and -63.6% for contribution margin. The sharper margin decline justified investigation, but operational checks found the offer in stock and the FBA-fee movement immaterial.

The implied contribution-margin rate also moved from 19.7% ($14,529.98 ÷ $73,631.43) to 14.4% ($5,281.59 ÷ $36,774.97), a 5.4-point deterioration. That rate calculation is more informative than margin dollars alone because it separates the effect of lower sales volume from weaker economics per revenue dollar.

05 / Scoring and action

Turn findings into a prioritized change log.

ScoreMeaningAction
0Broken or blockingFix before spending: suppression, no offer, stockout, wrong variation, compliance risk.
1Material weaknessPrioritize in the next controlled change cycle.
2Acceptable, improvableTest only with a defined hypothesis and success metric.
3StrongPreserve; avoid changing a working component without evidence.

Record the baseline, exact change, date, affected ASINs, expected mechanism, primary metric, guardrail metrics, and review window. A change without a measurement plan creates activity—not learning.

How to run the audit

Follow the dependency chain before scoring the creative.

Step 1 · Freeze the operating context

Record the ASIN, parent and child variation, marketplace, seller, fulfillment method, price, coupon, inventory position, and date of observation. Screenshots and exports should share the same review window where possible. Without that context, a later analyst cannot tell whether a content change, offer change, or stock event created the movement.

Step 2 · Clear retail-readiness blockers

Confirm that the exact child is active, purchasable, eligible for the intended offer, and deliverable within a competitive window. Test the shopper path rather than relying only on account status. If the offer is unavailable or another seller controls the featured offer, impressions and clicks may exist while the brand cannot reliably convert them. These are score-zero failures because funding more traffic cannot repair the checkout path.

Step 3 · Verify relevance with evidence

Build a query map that distinguishes product identity from attributes, audiences, problems, and use cases. Check whether priority terms are indexed and whether the title, bullets, category fields, and backend terms communicate the same product. Rank alone is not sufficient: a high position on four sampled terms, as seen in the archived audit, does not prove broad query coverage. It only argues against a collapse on those reviewed terms.

Step 4 · Audit conversion by shopper question

Read the page in decision order. The main image and title must establish identity and offer; supplemental images should resolve size, configuration, use, compatibility, proof, and predictable objections; A+ content should deepen rather than repeat. Compare the live search-results page because click-through depends on relative price, rating, coupon, delivery, and thumbnail—not on the listing in isolation.

Step 5 · Reconcile performance and economics

In the July-to-August 2024 snapshot, units fell from 4,105 to 2,049 and revenue from $73,631.43 to $36,774.97. Applying (August − July) ÷ July produces roughly -50.1% for both. Contribution margin fell from $14,529.98 to $5,281.59, or -63.6%, so the commercial problem was not only lower volume. The implied contribution-margin rate fell from 19.7% to 14.4%, a 5.4-percentage-point deterioration that requires a price, ad-cost, fee, discount, reimbursement, and mix bridge.

Interpretation rule: treat every metric as a locator, not a verdict. Lower impressions point toward visibility; lower click-through toward the search-result offer; lower conversion toward fit, trust, price, or page communication; lower margin rate toward unit economics. Each branch still requires corroborating evidence.

Repeatable checklist

Convert the audit into a controlled improvement cycle.

Document

Capture baseline dates, ASIN scope, offer state, inventory, price, traffic, conversion, margin, and current creative before changing anything.

Prioritize

Fix blocking retail-readiness issues first, then material relevance or conversion gaps, then optional optimizations. Preserve components already supported by evidence.

Hypothesize

Write the mechanism in one sentence: “Changing X should improve Y because Z.” Name a guardrail such as margin, return rate, or organic rank.

Isolate

Avoid simultaneous title, image, price, and bid changes when the objective is learning. If urgency requires a bundle, record every component.

Measure

Choose a review window suited to traffic and purchase latency. Compare like periods and retain the unchanged baseline.

Decide

Keep, revert, iterate, or escalate based on the primary metric, guardrails, and confidence—not preference for the new creative.
