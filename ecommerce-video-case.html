E-commerce · After Effects · Conversion

Features shoppers can see.

Five product videos finished in After Effects as conversion assets rather than passive content. Across the series I used shape layers, tracked typography, time remapping, masking, color correction and light-and-shadow compositing to turn raw demonstrations into clear buying evidence.

The conversion logic

Reduce uncertainty before asking for the sale.

01

Lead with the benefit

Each edit establishes one primary buying reason early, so the shopper does not need to decode a list of specifications.

02

Turn claims into proof

Weight, portability, adjustability and multiple configurations are demonstrated by people and product interaction.

03

Design for silent viewing

Benefit typography, visual sequencing and close-ups keep the story understandable when marketplace videos begin muted.

04

Support the decision

The edit prioritizes information that removes doubt: scale, setup, handling, storage and real-life use.
