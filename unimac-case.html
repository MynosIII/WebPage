UNIMAC Case · Product campaign

Turning industrial heat into a clear product story.

01 · Strategy

Designed around the buyer’s decision process.

The intended customer is a tradesperson, workshop owner, contractor, warehouse operator or agricultural user. They need reliable evidence—not decorative promises.

Heat

Will it produce enough heat and airflow for a demanding workspace?

Strength

Can its steel construction withstand an industrial environment?

Practicality

Is it portable, compact and easy to store between jobs?

Control

Can the airflow be directed and adjusted without a complex interface?

Fit

Will it work in the customer’s specific garage, workshop or work area?

Proof

Are the dimensions, weight and performance specifications easy to verify?

02 · Visual direction

Heat, strength and technical trust.

Orange communicates active performance. Black reinforces durability. White creates clarity in the technical panels. Bold condensed typography and short headlines make every benefit readable within seconds, including on mobile.

03 · Campaign sequence

One message per panel. One connected conversion story.

04 · Production workflow

Photography, compositing and graphic precision.

  1. 01

    Customer and category review

    Mapped the product’s features against buyer concerns and competitor conventions before designing any graphics.

  2. 02

    Sequence and photography planning

    Assigned one communication objective to each panel, then planned three-quarter, control, carrying and tilt views.

  3. 03

    Photoshop compositing

    Handled masking, metal retouching, perspective, contact shadows, ambient color and controlled heat spill for believable environments.

  4. 04

    Illustrator system

    Built the typography, icons, measurement lines, grids and specification tables as one reusable visual language.

  5. 05

    Verification and mobile QA

    Checked technical consistency, tested claims and reduced every panel to marketplace mobile size before export.

05 · Design integrity

Credibility is part of the creative.

Use verified language. “280 m³/h airflow” is more accurate than promising a guaranteed room-heating volume.

Connect features to outcomes. Adjustable frame becomes “direct heat where it is needed.” Compact dimensions become easier storage.

Keep the heat believable. Orange glow clarifies function, but excessive effects can misrepresent the physical product.

Design for the actual customer. Workshops and working environments reinforce relevance better than generic domestic lifestyle imagery.

Outcome

A cohesive image system that balances emotional relevance with practical proof.

The campaign guides buyers from recognition and interest through trust, understanding, technical validation and brand confidence.
