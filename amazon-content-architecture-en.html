Amazon Content · Conversion Design · Information Architecture

Each block answers one purchase question.

A framework to build product pages as decision systems. The title generates relevance, the bullets organize value, the gallery demonstrates, the video reduces uncertainty and A+ expands the story without repeating it.

Is it for me?What does it solve?Can I believe it?What option do I choose?Why now?
The content stack

From keyword to trust, without losing continuity.

The problem is not a lack of content. It is duplication: the same claim repeated in title, bullet, image and A+. Strong architecture assigns each surface a function and moves the buyer forward.

Five layers

A sequence, not a collection of assets.

01 Discovery

Qualification

Connect search language with a human description of the product. Prioritize relevance and intention, maintaining a logical reading.

Question: “Did I find what I was looking for?”
02 · Orientation

Bullets

They transform features into benefits and order the main arguments according to importance for the decision.

Question: “What does it do for me?”
03 Evidence

Gallery

The main image wins the click. The following demonstrate use, scale, fit, mechanisms and responses to real objections.

Question: “How do I know it works?”
04 · Understanding

Video

It shows sequences that an image cannot explain: assembly, movement, transformation, portability and user experience.

Question: “How is it used in real life?”
05 Confidence

A+ and variations

They deepen comparison, product system and option choice, without competing with the critical information of the first viewport.

Question: “Which is the correct option?”

Measurement loop

Design a hypothesis that can be read.

01

Detect friction

Use queries, reviews, competition and behavior to define what doubt blocks the purchase.

02

Assign surface

Choose the module capable of solving it: copy, image, video, variation or expanded content.

03

Change with intention

Document date, hypothesis and main metric before publishing a new version.

04

Read the system

Evaluate CTR, conversion, terms, returns and context; do not declare success by a single sign.

Result

Less repetition. More progress.

An effective product page doesn't try to say everything everywhere. Build a sequence where each module reduces a different uncertainty and gives the next an easier decision.
