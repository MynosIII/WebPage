02
Mathematically analyzed

Fill in gaps without inventing certainties

Many surveys publish only “positive” and “negative”; others separate each response into five levels. The model redistributes the missing totals using the structure observed in nearby measurements and retains the percentage from each survey. It is a descriptive imputation: it helps compare formats, but it does not replace the original data.

Modeled composition

How to read it
  1. Standardization. Each survey is taken to a comparable base of 100%.
  2. Imputation. If categories are missing, they are distributed according to close observed proportions.
  3. Aggregation. The results are averaged by month to reduce noise between firms.
  4. Smoothed. The line connects averages with a curve; does not add new observations.

How it was built

From API to searchable timeline

The starting point was the massive import of videos from YouTube channels using its API. Those results were combined with additional searches and registrations to cover appearances that were not hosted on the main channels.

  1. 01
    Matter

    Consultation of channels and recovery of titles, links and published videos.

  2. 02
    Extract

    Reading and normalization of dates present in titles or metadata.

  3. 03
    Enrich

    Manual or semi-automatic assignment of interviewer, program, medium and location.

  4. 04
    Consolidate

    Unification of sources, chronological order and control of repeated records.

CTR, conversion, sales and sentiment studies show what worked and what needs to change.

The difference matters

A political campaign disputes representation and power; A commercial campaign seeks to produce a business action. They are not equivalent, but both require understanding circulation, persuasion, segmentation and response.
