Intervention: I audited campaign and listing architecture, reorganized targeting, and coordinated content and media changes.

Working principle · Analytical Translator

Translating complex signals into decisions people can execute.

Analytical Translator describes how I work; it does not replace my role. I connect customer language, commercial data and execution so an insight ends in a concrete decision.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    I connect market, funnel, catalog, content, media and economics to find the actual constraint.

  2. 02

    Prioritize

    I rank opportunities by impact, evidence, effort and commercial risk.

  3. 03

    Implement

    I turn the hypothesis into a concrete intervention with defined ownership and scope.

  4. 04

    Measure

    I document baseline, outcome, period and limits; I separate correlation from causality.
