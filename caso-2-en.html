Business Intelligence & Profitability

The problem

The challenge was not only to sell more, but to understand whether the growth was really leaving room. In ecommerce, an increase in sales can hide increases in ad spend, fees, returns, discounts or logistics costs.

The work consisted of going from an isolated sales reading to a portfolio reading: product by product, margin by margin, and with a clear separation between healthy growth and artificial growth.

  • BI Cross reading of sales, ads and margin
  • profit Product profitability control
  • action Prioritization of products with real upside
Decisions

what did I do

I built a monthly performance reading to detect products where paid traffic was driving profitable sales and products where spending only disguised the result. This allowed offensive actions to be separated from defensive actions.

The criterion was to avoid changes due to anxiety. When a product was stable, there was no intervention. When the data showed session decline, CTR or margin, the stock was heading towards the specific bottleneck.

  • +220% Observed growth in sales during the period
  • Less noise Decisions by cause, not by intuition
  • Portfolio Prioritization according to margin and opportunity
