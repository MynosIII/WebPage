Business Intelligence & Profitability

The problem

The challenge was not only to sell more, but to understand whether growth was generating a healthy margin. In ecommerce, higher sales can conceal increases in ad spend, fees, returns, discounts or logistics costs.

The work moved from an isolated sales view to portfolio-level analysis: product by product, margin by margin, with a clear distinction between healthy and artificial growth.

  • BI Cross-analysis of sales, ads and margin
  • Profit Product-level profitability control
  • Action Prioritization of products with real upside
Decisions

What I did

I built a monthly performance reading to detect products where paid traffic was driving profitable sales and products where spending only disguised the result. This allowed offensive actions to be separated from defensive actions.

The principle was to avoid reactive changes. Stable products were left alone; when the data showed a decline in sessions, CTR or margin, the action targeted that specific bottleneck.

  • +220% Observed growth in sales during the period
  • Less noise Decisions by cause, not by intuition
  • Portfolio Prioritization according to margin and opportunity
