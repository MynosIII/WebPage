Product & Conversion Gallery

Visual redesign aimed at reducing doubts, explaining compatibility and converting technical features into perceived value.

Image design & CRO

The problem

Many technical products fail not because the user does not want them, but because they do not quickly understand if they are useful for their case. In categories with compatibility, measurements or installation, the image must function as a sales argument and as a filter for doubts.

The goal was to transform the gallery into a clear sequence: product identification, main benefit, compatibility, use, installation and visual proof of quality.

Intervention

what did I do

I reordered the visual communication so that each image fulfilled a function. The cover had to earn the click, the infographics had to answer objections and the secondary pieces had to reinforce technical confidence.

The design was aimed at quick reading, high contrast, visual hierarchy and less unnecessary decoration. The priority was for the shopper to understand the product before leaving the page.

  • -30% Estimated page bounce
  • +12% Conversion
  • More clarity Less technical friction

The hypothesis was that a clear image can fulfill the role of silent salesperson. It not only shows the product: it reduces uncertainty, accelerates comparison and improves the quality of traffic that reaches the purchase.
